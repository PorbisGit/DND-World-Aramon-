## Overview

*Hrimskar is a loose coalition of goliath tribes united by shared traditions, mutual survival, and the teachings of the Three Pillars. Life in the southern tundra is unforgiving, and its people believe only those who embrace hardship, community, and change can truly endure.*

## Culture

- Values: 
	- *Community, endurance, self-improvement, honour, and resilience.* Every goliath is encouraged to embody the Three Pillars throughout their life, striving to know when to stand firm like the Mountain, support others like the Flame, or embrace change like the Storm.
- Traditions/Festivals:
	- The first campfire of winter is lit in honour of **The Flame**, celebrating unity before the harshest season.
	- Climbs to sacred peaks are undertaken to reflect upon **The Mountain**.
	- Trials of endurance and combat are held during great storms to honour **The Storm**.
- Everyday Life: 
	- Daily life is guided by the Three Pillars. Elders teach that every hardship strengthens the Mountain, every act of kindness feeds the Flame, and every challenge carries the Storm's lesson. Rather than praying for guidance, most goliaths seek to live in balance with all three.
