## Basic Information

**Name:** [[Khazrim]]

**Government:** High Kingdom

**Leader:** High King Mukhac Dragonheart

**Capital:** Khazrim

**Primary Races:** [[Dwarves]]

**Languages:** Common, Dwarvish

**Religion(s):** [[Dwarven Pantheon]]

## Overview

*Khazrim is the greatest surviving dwarven kingdom, built atop the ruins of the ancient Stoneways that once united every major dwarven hold beneath Ogrin. While much of the underground empire has been lost to time, monsters, and collapse, its people believe reclaiming the Stoneways means reclaiming the very soul of dwarfkind.*

## History

- Founding:
	- Established upon the surviving entrance to the Stoneways following the collapse of the ancient dwarven empire.
- Major Historical Events:
	- Construction of the Stoneways.
	- Collapse of the underground empire.
	- Founding of modern Khazrim.
	- Rediscovery of several lost Stoneway sections.
- Current Situation:
	- While many seek to restore the Stoneways and honour the ancestors who built them, a growing movement believes dwarfkind must stop living in the past and forge a new future.

## Government & Military

- Political Structure: 
	- A hereditary monarch advised by clan elders, guild masters, and the High Priesthood.
- Military Overview:
	- Highly disciplined heavy infantry supported by master smiths, engineers, and fortress defenders.
- Laws or Unique Customs:
	- Every dwarf contributes to their clan before themselves.
	- Every crafted work bears its maker's mark.
	- Ancestors are consulted before major decisions.

## Culture

- Values:
	- Legacy, craftsmanship, duty, perseverance, and honour.
- Traditions/Festivals
	- Every dwarf records their lineage.
	- Weapons are passed through generations.
	- Clan feasts honour both the living and the dead.
	- Young dwarves undertake a journey into the Stoneways.
- Everyday Life
	- Daily life revolves around family, clan, and craft. A dwarf's reputation is earned through the quality of their work and the legacy they leave behind, whether through their creations, their service, or the family they raise.
## Diplomacy
 
- Allies:
- Rivals: Other Dwavern Nations/Factions that occupy the Stoneways
- Current Conflicts: 
	- While expeditions continue to reclaim the Stoneways, growing political tension exists between traditionalists seeking to restore the old empire and reformists who believe dwarfkind must abandon its obsession with the past.
