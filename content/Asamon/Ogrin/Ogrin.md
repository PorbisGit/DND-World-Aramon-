# ## Basic Information

**Name:** [[Ogrin]]

**Type:** Continent

**Region/Continent:** Material Plane

**Population:** Millions

**Primary Inhabitants:** Humans, Dwarves, Halflings, Goliaths

## Overview

_Ogrin is one of the great continents of Asamon and the ancestral homeland of humanity, dwarves, halflings and goliaths. Dominated by the immense Worldspine Mountains that stretch from the frozen southern reaches of the continent to its warm northern frontiers, Ogrin is a land of fertile plains, ancient forests, prosperous kingdoms and enduring traditions. For centuries its peoples flourished through trade, diplomacy and mutual respect, becoming one of the political and economic centres of the known world._

_That age of stability has begun to fracture. The death of the King of [[Stolan]] shattered decades of peace between the human kingdoms, plunging [[Stolan]], [[Stanosta]] and [[Vibet]] into a devastating war. While [[Khazrim]], [[Hrimskar]] and [[The Hearthland Moot]] remain outside the conflict, each now watches events with growing concern as the mysterious demonic curse spreads across the continent and demons begin appearing where they should not._

## History

**Origin:**

- During the Age of Dominion, Ogrin was ruled by mighty dwarven kingdoms beneath the Worldspine Mountains and powerful giant realms that dominated much of the surface. Together they shaped the continent through vast roads, immense fortresses and monumental works whose ruins still endure today.
- The collapse of these ancient powers at the end of the Age of Dominion transformed Ogrin forever. As the giants disappeared and the dwarves withdrew into the safety of [[Khazrim]], humanity, halflings and goliaths gradually rose to establish the nations that now define the continent.

**Notable Events:**

- Rise of the Kingdom of [[Stolan]].
- Founding of [[Stanosta]].
- Formation of the [[The Hearthland Moot]].
- Unification of the clans of [[Hrimskar]].
- Establishment of modern [[Khazrim]].
- Death of the King of [[Stolan]], leading to the War of the Three Kingdoms.

**Current State:**

- [[Stolan]], [[Stanosta]] and [[Vibet]] remain locked in a devastating war.
- [[Khazrim]], [[Hrimskar]] and [[The Hearthland Moot]] continue to preserve their neutrality while preparing for an uncertain future.
- The mysterious demonic curse continues to spread throughout the continent.
- Reports of demons appearing across Ogrin have become increasingly common.

## Culture

**Lifestyle:**

- Ogrin is home to four of Asamon's great peoples, each having developed their own cultures while remaining closely connected through centuries of trade, diplomacy and shared history. Merchant cities, sacred citadels, mountain halls, clan settlements and independent villages together create one of the most culturally diverse continents in the world.

**Traditions:**

- The Annual Moot of the halflings.
- Clan gatherings of [[Hrimskar]].
- The great smithing traditions of [[Khazrim]].
- Seasonal festivals celebrated throughout the human kingdoms.
