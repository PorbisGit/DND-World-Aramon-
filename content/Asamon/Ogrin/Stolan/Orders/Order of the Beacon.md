## Basic Information

**Name:** Order of the Beacon

**Type:** Religious Order

**Location:** [[Stolan]]

**Leader(s):** Grand Beacon 

**Associated Culture/Race:** Primarily [[Humans]]

## Overview

_The Order of the Beacon is a religious order devoted to redemption, believing that no soul is beyond salvation. It welcomes criminals, outcasts, and those seeking a second chance, offering them purpose through service to the kingdom and the gods._

## History

**Origin:** Founded to offer redemption to those who sought to atone for past mistakes through faithful service.

**Major Events:** Earned the personal favour of the late king after repeatedly defending villages and refugees despite their reputation.

**Current Situation:** While respected by many common folk, the Order faces growing political pressure from more influential religious orders that view its methods with suspicion.

## Beliefs & Identity

**Core Values:** Redemption, Compassion, Service, Humility.

**Traditions:** Members abandon their former titles and are judged only by the lives they choose to lead.

**Reputation:** Respected by the people but often looked down upon by more prestigious orders.

**Common Symbols:** A lit beacon upon a hill.

## Structure

**Leadership:** Led by the Grand Beacon and senior mentors who guide new members.

**Ranks/Hierarchy:** Initiate → Warden → Beacon → High Beacon → Grand Beacon

## Resources & Influence

**Strengths:** Fiercely loyal members, strong community ties, and unmatched determination.

**Resources:** Monasteries, shelters, training halls, and charitable networks.

**Areas of Influence:** Throughout [[Stolan]], particularly among poorer communities.

**Allies:** The Crown, [[Order of the Crown]], local communities, and charitable organisations.

**Enemies/Rivals:** Several influential religious orders that question the Order's acceptance of former criminals.

## Goals & Conflicts

**Current Goals:** Redeem those seeking a second chance while protecting the innocent.

**Internal Problems:** Some members struggle to leave behind their former lives.

**External Threats:** Political opposition from rival orders and growing distrust during the ongoing war.
