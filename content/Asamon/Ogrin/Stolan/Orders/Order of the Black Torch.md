## Basic Information

**Name:** Order of the Black Torch

**Type:** Hunter Order

**Location:** [[Stolan]]

**Leader(s):** High Inquisitor

**Associated Culture/Race:** All

## Overview

*The Order of the Black Torch is Stolan's foremost defence against supernatural threats, tasked with hunting demons, devils, monsters, cults and other evils that threaten the kingdom. Answering directly to the Crown, its members operate beyond the duties of ordinary soldiers or knights, venturing wherever darkness takes root. Feared for their ruthless methods and unwavering resolve, the Order is often regarded as the kingdom's last resort—called upon only when others have already failed.*

## History

- **Origin:**
	- Founded after a series of devastating supernatural disasters demonstrated that conventional armies were ill-equipped to confront the horrors lurking beyond civilisation.

- **Major Historical Events:**
	- Destroyed numerous demonic cults before they could take root within the kingdom.
	- Contained outbreaks of powerful curses and supernatural plagues.
	- Played a critical role in defending Stolan against fiends, undead and other unnatural threats throughout its history.

- **Current Situation:**
	- With the curse spreading and reports of demons appearing with increasing frequency, the Order has expanded its operations across the kingdom, often acting before other Orders even recognise a threat exists.

## Beliefs & Identity

- **Core Values:**
	- Resolve
	- Vigilance
	- Sacrifice
	- Duty
	- Survival

- **Traditions:**
	- Members are trained to identify, contain and destroy supernatural threats by whatever means are necessary.
	- Every hunter is expected to master knowledge of curses, fiends, monsters, relics and forbidden magic alongside their chosen style of combat.
	- The Order welcomes individuals from all walks of life, valuing experience and determination over birth or social standing.
	- Rather than enforcing a single fighting style, members are encouraged to develop whatever skills best prepare them to face the horrors they hunt.

- **Reputation:**
	- Widely respected for protecting the kingdom from threats few others are willing to face.
	- Equally feared for the brutal decisions they are sometimes forced to make, with many believing the Order sacrifices too much of its humanity in pursuit of its duty.
	- Among the common folk, a saying has endured for generations:
		- *"The Black Torch doesn't come to save villages. It comes to decide whether they're worth saving."*

- **Common Symbols:**
	- A black torch bearing a silver flame.

- **Motto:**
	- *"The fire decides."*

## Goals & Conflicts

- **Current Goals:**
	- Eliminate supernatural threats before they can spread.
	- Investigate the growing number of demonic appearances across the kingdom.
	- Contain and, where possible, understand the curse.

- **Internal Problems:**
	- Prolonged exposure to supernatural horrors leaves many members emotionally detached and increasingly isolated from ordinary society.
	- Debate continues within the Order over how far its methods can be justified before they become as dangerous as the evils they hunt.

- **External Threats:**
	- The demonic curse.
	- Rising demonic activity.
	- Expanding cults.
	- Ancient evils awakening throughout the kingdom.
