## Basic Information

**Name:** Order of the Argent Scale

**Type:** Chivalric Order

**Location:** [[Stolan]]

**Leader(s):** Grandmaster 

**Associated Culture/Race:** Primarily [[Humans]] and [[Dragonborn]]

## Overview

*The Order of the Argent Scale is one of Stolan's most prestigious Orders, entrusted with safeguarding the kingdom's prosperity and stability. Closely associated with the Silver Dragon that has protected the capital for centuries, its members defend the trade, institutions and relationships upon which the kingdom's wealth depends. While the Order serves Stolan above all else, generations spent alongside the dragon have left its traditions strongly influenced by the ideals of courage and protection embodied by [[Dravaryn]].*

## History

- **Origin:**
	- Founded after the Silver Dragon settled within Stolan's capital, originally to assist in protecting the city's merchants, trade and growing prosperity.

- **Major Historical Events:**
	- Expanded alongside Stolan's rise as one of the wealthiest kingdoms in the region.
	- Successfully uncovered numerous smuggling operations, espionage networks and conspiracies that threatened the kingdom's economy.
	- Formed a lasting partnership with the Silver Dragon, whose wisdom continues to shape the Order's traditions.

- **Current Situation:**
	- With war disrupting trade across the Three Kingdoms and corruption taking root within Stolan, the Order has become increasingly active in protecting the nation's economic stability and uncovering hidden threats before they can endanger the kingdom.

## Beliefs & Identity

- **Core Values:**
	- Vigilance
	- Integrity
	- Prosperity
	- Courage
	- Service

- **Traditions:**
	- Members are taught that prosperity cannot exist without security, and that the greatest threats are often those hidden beneath the surface.
	- Dragonborn are commonly found within the Order, with many viewing service alongside the Silver Dragon as one of the highest honours they can achieve.
	- Although not a religious organisation, centuries of serving beside the Silver Dragon have deeply influenced the Order's customs and philosophy, particularly the virtues of courage, wisdom and protection associated with [[Dravaryn]].

- **Reputation:**
	- Regarded as trustworthy protectors of Stolan's prosperity and among the kingdom's finest investigators of economic and political threats.
	- Their presence is often welcomed by merchants and guilds, but feared by smugglers, corrupt officials and foreign agents.

- **Common Symbols:**
	- A silver dragon's scale balanced upon a merchant's scale.

## Resources & Influence

- **Strengths:**
	- Economic security
	- Counter-espionage
	- Intelligence gathering
	- Protection of trade
	- Diplomatic security

- **Resources:**
	- Chapter houses throughout [[Stolan]].

- **Allies:**

	- Stolan Merchant Guilds.

- **Enemies/Rivals:**

	- Counterfeiters.

## Goals & Conflicts

- **Current Goals:**
	- Protect Stolan's prosperity and economic stability.
	- Uncover hidden threats to the kingdom's trade and institutions.
	- Safeguard major trade routes, merchant guilds and diplomatic relations.
	- Continue serving alongside the Silver Dragon in defence of the capital.

- **Internal Problems:**
	- Maintaining political neutrality despite pressure from influential merchant houses.
	- Balancing secrecy with the trust expected of one of Stolan's most respected Orders.

- **External Threats:**
	- Espionage.
	- Economic sabotage.
	- Corruption.
	- Organised crime.
	- War disrupting commerce across the Three Kingdoms.
