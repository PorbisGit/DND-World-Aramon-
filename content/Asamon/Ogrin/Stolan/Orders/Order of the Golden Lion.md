## Basic Information

**Name:** Order of the Golden Lion

**Type:** Noble Order

**Location:** [[Stolan]]

**Leader(s):** Grand Chancellor

**Associated Culture/Race:** Primarily Humans

## Overview

*The Order of the Golden Lion is Stolan's foremost institution of governance, diplomacy and noble education. Dedicated to supporting the kingdom's governors and noble houses, its members are renowned for their intellect, political insight and refined leadership. While respected for producing many of the kingdom's finest administrators and diplomats, the Order's growing pride has led some within its ranks to believe they are not merely best suited to advise the kingdom—but to rule it.*

## History

- **Origin:**
	- Founded to educate the kingdom's nobility and provide governors with capable advisers, administrators and diplomats.

- **Major Historical Events:**
	- Established academies that became the standard for educating Stolan's future governors and noble heirs.
	- Negotiated numerous treaties and diplomatic agreements that strengthened the kingdom's influence.
	- Played a vital role in organising Stolan's provincial administration.

- **Current Situation:**
	- The Order remains one of the most influential institutions within Stolan. While the majority faithfully serve the kingdom, rumours persist that a powerful faction within the Order is quietly expanding its political influence, though no evidence has ever directly linked its actions back to the organisation.

## Beliefs & Identity

- **Core Values:**
	- Leadership
	- Wisdom
	- Duty
	- Honour
	- Excellence

- **Traditions:**
	- Members undergo years of rigorous education in diplomacy, governance, history, economics, military strategy, law and etiquette.
	- Many of Stolan's future governors, diplomats and noble heirs receive part of their education through the Order's academies before assuming positions of authority.
	- Members are expected to conduct themselves with dignity, discipline and unwavering confidence, believing a leader should inspire respect through both knowledge and character.

- **Reputation:**
	- Regarded as some of the kingdom's most educated and capable individuals, second only to the Order of the Crown in prestige.
	- Their confidence and refinement have earned them a reputation for pride and arrogance, particularly among the lower classes and rival Orders.

- **Common Symbols:**
	- A golden lion sitting proudly beneath a crown.

## Goals & Conflicts

	- Educate the next generation of Stolan's leaders.
	- Strengthen the kingdom through effective governance and diplomacy.
	- Support the nobility in administering their lands and serving the Crown.

- **Internal Problems:**
	- A secretive faction within the Order believes the nobility are inherently destined to rule and has begun manipulating political affairs to expand noble influence throughout the kingdom.
	- Although many suspect the faction's existence, no evidence has ever been uncovered linking its activities directly to the Order.

- **External Threats:**
