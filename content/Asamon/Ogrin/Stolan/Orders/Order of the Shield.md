## Basic Information

**Name:** Order of the Shield

**Type:** Religious Order

**Location:** [[Stolan]]

**Leader(s):** High Marshal

**Associated Culture/Race:** Primarily [[Humans]]

## Overview

_The Order of the Shield is responsible for maintaining law, protecting the innocent, and enforcing the Crown's justice throughout [[Stolan]]. As the kingdom's largest religious order, its members serve in every city, town, and village, offering one of the few paths by which commoners can rise to positions of influence through service alone._

## History

**Origin:** Founded to uphold the king's peace and provide a unified force capable of protecting communities across the kingdom.

**Major Events:** Established a permanent network of garrisons, watch posts, and courthouses throughout [[Stolan]], bringing royal law to even the most remote settlements.

**Current Situation:** The ongoing war and the spread of the demonic curse have stretched the Order thin, while increasing political pressure has forced its leaders to balance serving the people with obeying the Crown and the kingdom's higher authorities.

## Beliefs & Identity

**Core Values:** Duty, Justice, Service, Discipline.

**Traditions:** Every member swears an oath to protect the people before themselves, regardless of status or wealth.

**Reputation:** Dependable protectors respected by most common folk, though some see them as the public face of increasingly strict royal law.

**Common Symbols:** A silver shield beneath a radiant crown.

## Structure

**Leadership:** Led by the High Marshal, who answers directly to the King and the kingdom's highest judicial authority.

**Ranks/Hierarchy:** Recruit → Guard → Sergeant → Captain → Marshal → High Marshal

## Resources & Influence

**Strengths:** Kingdom-wide presence, disciplined personnel, and unrivalled local knowledge.

**Resources:** Garrisons, watchtowers, armouries, courthouses, and patrol stations.

**Areas of Influence:** Every settlement and major roadway throughout [[Stolan]].

**Allies:** The Crown, provincial governors, [[Order of the Crown]]

**Enemies/Rivals:** Criminal organisations, raiders, and those who threaten public order.

## Goals & Conflicts

**Current Goals:** Maintain law and order while protecting the people during the ongoing war.

**Internal Problems:** Increasing workloads, limited manpower, and balancing the needs of the people with the demands of the Crown and influential Orders.

**External Threats:** The demonic curse, civil unrest, organised crime, and the effects of the war.
