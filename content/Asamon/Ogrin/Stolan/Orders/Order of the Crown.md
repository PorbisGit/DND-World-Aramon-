## Basic Information

**Name:** Order of the Crown

**Type:** Religious Order

**Location:** [[Stolan]]

**Leader(s):** Lord Commander

**Associated Culture/Race:** Primarily Humans

## Overview

_The Order of the Crown serves as the personal guard of the King and the highest authority among Stolan's Great Orders. Limited to only seven members at any one time, they are handpicked for their loyalty, wisdom, judgement, and exceptional ability, answering only to the Crown while ensuring every other Order remains faithful to the kingdom._

## History

**Origin:** Founded by the first King of [[Stolan]] to ensure no individual or Order would ever stand above the Crown.

**Major Events:** For centuries the Order has protected the royal bloodline, ended internal rebellions, and intervened whenever an Order exceeded its authority.

**Current Situation:** Following the death of the previous king, the Order remains fiercely loyal to the Crown, though its members are increasingly forced to mediate disputes between ambitious Orders while protecting the inexperienced young monarch.

## Beliefs & Identity

**Core Values:** Loyalty, Duty, Wisdom, Justice.

**Traditions:** Every member swears absolute loyalty to the Crown, surrendering all former allegiances upon joining the Order.

**Reputation:** Universally respected and quietly feared, with few willing to question their authority.

**Common Symbols:** A golden crown resting upon a silver shield.

## Structure

**Leadership:** Led by the Lord Commander, with the remaining six members serving as equals beneath their command.

**Ranks/Hierarchy:** Candidate → Crown Warden → Lord Commander

## Resources & Influence

**Strengths:** Seven of the kingdom's greatest warriors, unwavering loyalty, and authority over every other Order.

**Resources:** The Royal Armoury, the Crown Archives, and unrestricted access to the King's authority.

**Areas of Influence:** The Royal Court and all of [[Stolan]] through the authority of the Crown.

**Allies:** The King and the Great Orders.

**Enemies/Rivals:** Enemies of the Crown and those who seek to undermine the kingdom.

## Goals & Conflicts

**Current Goals:** Protect the Crown, preserve the kingdom's stability, and ensure every Great Order remains loyal to its sworn purpose.

**Internal Problems:** Balancing absolute loyalty to the Crown with the growing instability surrounding the young king.

**External Threats:** Political manipulation by ambitious Orders, the ongoing war, and the spread of the demonic curse.
