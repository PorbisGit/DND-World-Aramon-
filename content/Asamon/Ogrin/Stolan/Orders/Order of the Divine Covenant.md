## Basic Information

**Name:** Order of the Divine Covenant

**Type:** Religious Order

**Location:** [[Stolan]]

**Leader(s):** High Pontiff 

**Associated Culture/Race:** Primarily [[Humans]]

## Overview

*The Order of the Divine Covenant is the spiritual heart of Stolan, serving the Solari through worship, scholarship and guidance. Responsible for overseeing the kingdom's temples, preserving sacred knowledge and advising both the Crown and its people, the Order stands as one of the oldest and most influential institutions in the realm. Though united beneath a single covenant, differing interpretations of the gods' will have given rise to powerful schools of thought that quietly compete for influence within the Order.*

## History

- **Origin:**
	- Established alongside the founding of Stolan to preserve the teachings of the Solari and ensure that faith remained a guiding force within the kingdom.

- **Major Historical Events:**
	- Oversaw the construction of the Grand Cathedral, the spiritual centre of Stolan.
	- Founded and expanded the Crown Archives, preserving sacred scripture, royal history and countless records of the kingdom's past.
	- Helped establish temples dedicated to each of the Solari throughout the kingdom.

- **Current Situation:**
	- The Order continues to provide spiritual guidance across Stolan while studying the growing supernatural dangers threatening the realm. At the same time, disagreements over the proper role of faith in governing the kingdom have become increasingly common within its own ranks.

## Beliefs & Identity

- **Core Values:**
	- Faith
	- Wisdom
	- Compassion
	- Knowledge
	- Service

- **Traditions:**
	- Every member swears themselves to the Solari as a whole before choosing to devote their service primarily to one deity.
	- Members study theology, history, philosophy, divine magic and ancient scripture alongside their religious duties.
	- The Order safeguards relics, sacred texts and historical records for future generations.
	- Many priests, clerics, paladins, monks, scholars and archivists serve within its ranks.

- **The Solari:**
	- While united beneath the Divine Covenant, many members dedicate themselves to one of the seven Solari whose teachings best reflect their calling.
	- Temples devoted to individual gods can be found throughout Stolan wherever their influence is strongest, while the Grand Cathedral honours all seven equally.

- **The Noctari:**
	- The worship of the Noctari is forbidden throughout Stolan.
	- Their teachings, symbols and history are nevertheless studied by trusted members of the Order so that their cults and influence may be recognised and opposed.

- **Reputation:**
	- Respected throughout the kingdom for providing spiritual guidance, healing and education.
	- Considered one of the greatest centres of scholarship in Stolan due to its stewardship of the Crown Archives.
	- Though publicly united, many recognise that influential groups within the Order often disagree over how the will of the Solari should shape the kingdom's future.

- **Common Symbols:**
	- Seven interwoven rays surrounding a single radiant flame.

## Structure

- **Leadership:**
	- Led by the High Pontiff, supported by senior clergy representing the major branches of the Order.

- **Ranks/Hierarchy:**
	1. Acolyte
	2. Priest
	3. Canon
	4. High Canon
	5. High Pontiff

- **Communions:**
	- After swearing themselves to the Divine Covenant, many members devote their service primarily to one of the seven Solari.
	- These Communions oversee temples, traditions and religious practices associated with their chosen deity while remaining united beneath the Divine Covenant.

- **Internal Politics:**
	- Although united in purpose, several influential schools of thought compete peacefully for influence within the Order.
	- Most members broadly align themselves with one of these groups while still serving alongside those who hold different interpretations of the Solari's will.

## Goals & Conflicts

- **Current Goals:**
	- Preserve the teachings of the Solari.
	- Guide the people of Stolan through faith and wisdom.
	- Protect sacred relics and historical knowledge.
	- Support the kingdom against supernatural threats.

- **Internal Problems:**
	- Members often disagree over how the will of the Solari should influence the Crown and the kingdom.
	- While some believe faith should remain a guide for rulers, others believe the kingdom has grown too secular and should be governed more directly according to divine teachings.
	- The rarity of genuine divine communication has also led to debate over how new revelations should be recognised and interpreted.

- **External Threats:**
	- The growing influence of the Noctari.
	- Rising cult activity.
	- Supernatural corruption.
	- The increasing appearance of demons throughout the kingdom.
