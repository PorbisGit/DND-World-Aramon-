## Overview

_Stolan is the spiritual heart of humanity, where faith, duty and service guide every aspect of society. From the citadel-city of [[Highwatch]], generations of kings have ruled alongside the Great Religious Orders, building a kingdom renowned for its compassion, discipline and unwavering devotion to the gods. At the centre of Highwatch stand the Royal Palace, the Royal Cathedral and the Royal Archives, symbols of the kingdom's commitment to preserving both its faith and its history._

_The death of the beloved king shattered the unity he had spent decades fostering, leaving the young monarch to inherit a kingdom at war with [[Stanosta]] and [[Vibet]]. As conflict spreads and the mysterious demonic curse takes hold, the harmony that once defined Stolan has begun to fracture, allowing ambitious religious orders to shape the kingdom's future in the absence of the steady leadership once provided by the crown._

## History

- **Founding:**
    - Established around humanity's oldest holy sites, where the first kings united scattered realms beneath a single sacred crown.
- **Major Historical Events:**
    - Unification under the Crown.
    - Establishment of the Great Religious Orders.
    - Construction of Highwatch's Royal Cathedral and Royal Archives.
- **Current Situation:**
    - The death of the beloved king has sparked a devastating war with [[Stanosta]] and [[Vibet]].
    - The young king struggles to command the respect and authority once held by his father.
    - In the absence of strong royal leadership, several Great Religious Orders have begun exerting greater influence over the kingdom, each believing their vision offers the best future for Stolan.
    - The demonic curse continues to spread fear, unrest and fanaticism throughout the realm.

## Government & Military

- **Political Structure:** (Sacred Monarchy)
    1. King
    2. Leaders of the Great Religious Orders
    3. Governors
    4. Local Lords
- **Military Overview**:
    - Professional standing army.
    - Numerous paladins and other divine warriors.
    - Religious orders maintain their own specialised forces.
    - Provincial militias raised by governors.
    - Silver Dragon.
- **Laws or Unique Customs**:
    - Oaths sworn before the gods are legally binding.
    - Faith plays a central role in law, education and public life.
    - Religious orders hold significant authority throughout the kingdom.
    - The [[Order of the Divine Covenant]] answers only to the King and serves as guardian of the Royal Archives.

## Culture

- **Values**:
    - Faith
    - Duty
    - Order
    - Service
    - Compassion
- **Traditions/Festivals**:
    - Pilgrimages to sacred sites.
    - Seasonal holy festivals.
    - Public ceremonies honouring the gods.
    - Royal days of remembrance.
- **Everyday Life**:
    - Temples provide education, healing, charity and guidance for much of the population.
    - Most people honour the entire pantheon while holding one deity as their personal patron.
    - Many dedicate their lives to serving within one of the Great Religious Orders.
    - The Royal Archives within Highwatch's Cathedral preserve the history of humanity, safeguarded by the [[Order of the Divine Covenant]], whose members believe preserving truth is among the highest forms of service to both the Crown and the gods.
