## Overview

*Stanosta is a wealthy merchant kingdom whose fertile lands, bustling ports, and expansive trade networks once made it one of the richest nations in Ogrin. Following the death of its king, however, the kingdom has been plunged into war with [[Stolan]] and [[Vibet]], while corruption, greed, and a mysterious demonic curse threaten to unravel the prosperity it spent centuries building.*

## History

- **Founding:** United under a single crown by rival noble houses.
- **Major Historical Events:**
    - Expansion of the Great Trade Roads.
    - Rise of the Merchant Houses.
    - Establishment of Ogrin's largest merchant fleet.
- **Current Situation:** The king's death has sparked a devastating war with [[Stolan]] and [[Vibet]], while corruption and a spreading demonic curse continue to destabilise the kingdom.

	2. Dukes
	3. Counts
	4. Barons
	5. Local Lords
- **Military Overview**:
	- Professional standing army.
	- Powerful merchant fleet adapted for naval warfare.
	- Levies raised by dukes and local lords.

- **Laws or Unique Customs**:
	- Noble titles carry responsibilities as well as privileges.

	- Trade contracts are legally enforceable across the kingdom.
