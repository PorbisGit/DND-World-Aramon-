## Overview

*Stanosta is a wealthy merchant kingdom whose fertile lands, bustling ports and vast trade networks have made it one of the richest and most influential nations in Ogrin. At its heart lies [[Alderport]], the largest human city in the world, where commerce, culture and opportunity converge. Long regarded as an age of prosperity and ambition, the kingdom now faces an uncertain future as war, corruption and a mysterious demonic curse slowly erode the foundations of its success.*

## History

- **Founding:**
    - United under a single crown by rival noble houses after centuries of competition over the prosperous trade routes surrounding Alderport.
- **Major Historical Events:**
    - Expansion of the Great Trade Roads.
    - Rise of the Merchant Houses.
    - Establishment of Ogrin's largest merchant fleet.
- **Current Situation:**
    - The king's death has sparked a devastating war with [[Stolan]] and [[Vibet]].
    - Wartime disruption has weakened trade, allowing corruption and organised crime to flourish.
    - The demonic curse continues to spread uncertainty throughout the kingdom, threatening both commerce and public order.

## Government & Military

- **Political Structure:** (Feudal Monarchy)
    1. King
    2. Dukes
    3. Counts
    4. Barons
    5. Local Lords
- **Military Overview**:
    - Professional standing army.
    - Powerful merchant fleet adapted for naval warfare.
    - Levies raised by dukes and local lords.
    - Mercenary companies.
    - Silver Dragon.
- **Laws or Unique Customs**:
    - Noble titles carry responsibilities as well as privileges.
    - Royal protection extends to licensed merchants and recognised trade guilds.
    - Trade contracts are legally enforceable throughout the kingdom.
    - Foreign merchants are encouraged to settle and conduct business within Stanosta.
