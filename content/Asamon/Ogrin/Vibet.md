## Overview

_Vibet is a disciplined human kingdom renowned for its magical academies, military expertise and relentless pursuit of knowledge. Built upon the belief that every mystery can eventually be understood, the kingdom has become humanity's foremost centre for arcane research, producing many of the world's greatest scholars, battle mages and specialists. As the demonic curse continues to spread, Vibet has dedicated enormous resources to finding a cure, believing humanity's future may depend upon it._

## History

- **Founding:**
    - Established upon the ancient volcanic highlands where early human scholars discovered unusually rich concentrations of magical energy and rare minerals.
- **Major Historical Events:**
    - Founding of the Grand Arcane Academy.
    - Unification of the great academies beneath the First Director.
    - Establishment of the Arcane Accord, regulating magical research throughout the kingdom.
- **Current Situation:**
    - The war against [[Stolan]] and [[Stanosta]] continues, though Vibet's leadership views survival and long-term stability as greater priorities than territorial conquest.
    - Vast resources are devoted to researching the demonic curse, whose continued spread is believed to threaten the future of humanity.
    - Illegal magical experimentation has become increasingly common as scholars seek discoveries beyond the limits imposed by the Arcane Accord.

## Government & Military

- **Political Structure:** (Scholarchic Oligarchy)
    1. First Director
    2. The Directorate
    3. Provincial Directors
    4. City Governors
- **Military Overview**:
    - Professional standing army.
    - Extensive corps of battle mages.
    - Arcane engineers and rune specialists.
    - Highly organised logistical divisions.
    - Specialist military academies.
- **Laws or Unique Customs**:
    - Dangerous magical research is strictly regulated.
    - Human experimentation and unsanctioned summoning are among the kingdom's greatest crimes.
    - Magical practitioners must be licensed through recognised academies.
    - Achievement and expertise are valued above birth or noble lineage.

## Culture

- **Values**:
    - Knowledge
    - Discipline
    - Preparation
    - Competence
    - Progress
- **Traditions/Festivals**:
    - Graduation ceremonies for the great academies.
    - Annual symposiums where scholars present their greatest discoveries.
    - Public demonstrations of magical achievement.
- **Everyday Life**:
    - Education is considered one of the kingdom's highest callings.
    - Magical aptitude is encouraged, though all forms of dangerous research are carefully monitored.
    - Citizens are taught that understanding the world is humanity's greatest defence against extinction.
    - Many aspire to become scholars, specialists or researchers serving the kingdom's academies.
