## Overview

*Rathuun is the homeland of the orcs, forged not through inheritance but through perseverance. Once a barren wasteland of jagged stone, deadly valleys and unforgiving wilderness, generations of orcs transformed it into a thriving realm through determination, unity and an enduring respect for the land. While its people value strength, they believe true strength exists to protect others and leave the world better than it was found. Having spent much of their history simply surviving, Rathuun now stands at a crossroads as it seeks to define the role it will play in a rapidly changing world.*

## History

- **Founding:**
	- After leaving the Yssar, the first orcs settled within a harsh and seemingly uninhabitable land. Rather than abandon it, they endured, slowly transforming it into the homeland that would become Rathuun.
- **Major Historical Events:**
	- The Exodus from the Yssar.
	- Founding of Koruun.
	- The Restoration of the Great Rivers.
	- Recognition of the First Druids.
- **Current Situation:**
	- Rathuun enjoys a long period of stability and prosperity after centuries of hardship.
	- While relations with the Elven Ascendancy remain peaceful, many watch its growing influence with caution.
	- Having finally secured its future, the nation now debates how it should shape the future of the world rather than simply survive within it.

## Government & Military

- **Political Structure:**
	- Guardian
	- Guardian Assembly
	- City Representatives
	- Regional Councils
- **Military Overview:**
	- Highly disciplined professional warriors supported by experienced scouts, druids and monster riders.
	- Defensive fortifications make full use of Rathuun's natural terrain.
	- Cities are designed to reinforce one another, creating one of the hardest nations in the world to invade.
	- Military service is viewed as a duty of protection rather than conquest.
- **Laws or Unique Customs:**
	- The Guardian may be challenged through a Trial of Leadership should they no longer be considered fit to lead.
	- Every citizen is encouraged to contribute to the prosperity of future generations.
	- Great achievements are preserved through stories, monuments and songs.
	- Leadership is considered a responsibility, never a privilege.

## Culture

- **Values:**
	- Responsibility
	- Perseverance
	- Freedom
	- Community
	- Legacy
- **Traditions/Festivals:**
	- Arrival Day, commemorating the first settlers of Rathuun.
	- Festivals celebrating the nation's greatest heroes.
	- Public retelling of heroic deeds to inspire future generations.
	- Seasonal gatherings honouring the balance between civilisation and nature.
- **Everyday Life:**
	- Orcs believe every generation should leave Rathuun stronger than it found it. Heroes are remembered not simply for their strength in battle, but for how they improved the lives of others through leadership, discovery, craftsmanship or service. Druidic magic is common throughout Rathuun, having emerged naturally as generations of orcs learned to live alongside the land rather than struggle against it. Their cities spread across the landscape instead of dominating it, blending into the valleys, rivers and forests that their ancestors fought so hard to cultivate.
