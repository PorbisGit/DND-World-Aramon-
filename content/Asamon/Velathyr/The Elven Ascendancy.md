## Overview

*The Elven Ascendancy is the largest and only empire in the world, ruling much of [[Velathyr]] from Elarion, the ancient capital of the Yssar. Founded by elves who once served as slaves beneath their creators, the empire has spent millennia rebuilding the world they inherited, believing it is their responsibility to preserve civilisation, protect the natural balance, and safeguard the greatest relics of history. While diplomacy remains its preferred path, the Ascendancy will not hesitate to intervene when it believes the stability of the world is at risk. To many they are wise custodians; to others, they are beginning to resemble the very empire they swore never to become.*

## History

- **Founding:**
	- Following the fall of the Yssar, the liberated elves established their capital within the ancient seat of their former masters, vowing to build a civilisation founded upon stewardship rather than domination.
- **Major Historical Events:**
	- Fall of the Yssar.
	- Founding of the Elven Ascendancy.
	- Formation of the Protectorates of [[The Artisanate]] and [[Umbryss]].
	- Establishment of lasting peace with [[Rathuun]].
- **Current Situation:**
	- The empire continues to safeguard much of [[Velathyr]] while balancing the autonomy of its protectorates.
	- Increasing resources have been devoted to studying [[Aidrix]]'s Living Crystal, creating growing political division throughout the empire.
	- Although most elves reject the legacy of the Yssar, many fear the empire's expanding authority and pursuit of magical advancement are leading it down an increasingly familiar path.

## Government & Military

- **Political Structure:**
	- High Empress
	- Imperial Court
	- Noble Houses
	- Regional Protectorates
	- The Emissaries
- **Military Overview:**
	- One of the largest professional armies in the world.
	- Elite battlemages and magical support divisions.
	- Highly trained rangers defending the ancient forests and imperial frontiers.
	- Powerful navy protecting Velathyr's coasts and trade routes.
	- The Emissaries serve as the direct representatives of the High Empress, overseeing diplomacy, investigations, protectorates, military operations and matters of imperial importance across the Ascendancy.
- **Laws or Unique Customs:**
	- Ancient forests and sacred sites are protected by Imperial Law.
	- Magical research is encouraged under careful oversight.
	- Service to the empire is regarded as one of the highest honours.
	- Access to the World Tree is strictly controlled to preserve the balance between the Material Plane and the Feywild.

## Culture

- **Values:**
	- Stewardship
	- Harmony
	- Knowledge
	- Responsibility
	- Excellence
- **Traditions/Festivals:**
	- Seasonal festivals celebrating the changing forests.
	- Pilgrimages to the World Tree.
	- Public remembrance of the fall of the Yssar.
	- Ceremonies marking the beginning of each new century.
- **Everyday Life:**
	- Most elves live peaceful lives centred around family, scholarship, artistry and the natural world. They are taught from a young age that civilisation is not a privilege but a responsibility entrusted to them by history. While the vast majority genuinely seek to preserve peace and harmony, growing debate surrounds the empire's increasing willingness to intervene beyond its borders and its continued experimentation with ancient powers. Whenever difficult decisions are made, an uncomfortable silence often hangs over those present—a quiet reminder of the civilisation whose throne they now occupy.
