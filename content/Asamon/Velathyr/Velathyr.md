## Overview

_Velathyr is the largest and most influential continent in [[Asamon]], serving as the heartland of modern elven civilisation. It is home to [[The Elven Ascendancy]], [[Rathuun]], [[The Artisanate]] and [[Umbryss]], whose histories remain inseparable through the legacy of the [[Yssar]]. From the towering canopy of [[The World Tree]] to the endless caverns beneath [[Umbryss]], the continent is shaped by stewardship, innovation, memory and renewal. Though its nations rarely wage open war, ancient relics, political rivalries and the weight of history continue to influence the future of Velathyr._

## History

**Origin:**

- Velathyr was once the heartland of the [[Yssar]], whose empire dominated much of the known world before its eventual collapse. Following their downfall, new civilisations rose upon their foundations, eventually giving birth to [[The Elven Ascendancy]], [[Rathuun]], [[The Artisanate]] and [[Umbryss]].

**Notable Events:**

- Rise of the [[Yssar]]
- Fall of the [[Yssar]]
- Founding of [[The Elven Ascendancy]]
- Building of [[Rathuun]]
- Founding of [[The Artisanate]]
- Establishment of [[Umbryss]]
- Growth of [[The World Tree]] into one of the greatest wonders of [[Asamon]]

**Current State:**

- Velathyr remains the political, cultural and economic centre of [[Asamon]].
- Centuries of cooperation have produced an era of remarkable peace and prosperity.
- Ancient [[Yssar]] ruins and relics continue to be uncovered across the continent.
- Modern disputes are more often fought through diplomacy, philosophy and political influence than open warfare.

## Culture

**Lifestyle:**

The peoples of Velathyr enjoy one of the highest standards of living in the known world. Trade, education and diplomacy flourish between its nations, while each civilisation has cultivated its own distinct identity. Despite their differences, nearly all share a profound respect for history and believe the lessons of the [[Yssar]] must never be forgotten.

**Traditions:**

- Pilgrimages to [[The World Tree]]
- Celebrations marking the founding of each nation
- Festivals honouring craftsmanship, scholarship and community
- Annual remembrance of the fall of the [[Yssar]]
