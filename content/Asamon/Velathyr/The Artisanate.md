## Overview

*The Artisanate is the homeland of the gnomes and one of the great nations of [[Velathyr]], renowned throughout the world for its ingenuity, scholarship and relentless pursuit of discovery. Nestled amongst towering forests, floating islands and rugged mountains overlooking the western sea, its cities are monuments to centuries of innovation, where ancient stone halls stand beside brass workshops, observatories and newly built airship docks. Once united with [[The Watchers]], the two peoples gradually diverged as the gnomes embraced invention, exploration and the pursuit of knowledge, while the Watchers dedicated themselves to preserving the world's most dangerous secrets. The catastrophic [[Soulfracture]] taught the Artisanate that every discovery carries responsibility, while the sighting of [[Xytherion]] consuming a distant star through the [[Astral Eye]] forever changed their understanding of the universe. Today, the Artisanate continues to shape civilisation through engineering, astronomy and exploration, while the scholars of [[The Last Equation]] quietly work to preserve life itself from threats that could one day bring about its end.*

## History

- **Founding:**
    - Following the fall of the Yssar, the gnomes established their own nation amongst the forests and mountains of western Velathyr, where curiosity, craftsmanship and scholarship flourished. Though once united with the people who would become [[The Watchers]], the growing pursuit of invention and exploration gradually led the two societies down very different paths.
- **Major Historical Events:**
    - Founding of the Artisanate.
    - The Great Age of Exploration.
    - Invention of the compass.
    - Development of modern navigation through astronomy.
    - Construction of the Astral Eye.
    - Discovery of Xytherion after witnessing it consume a distant star.
    - The Soulfracture.
    - Establishment of The Last Equation.
    - Invention of the first successful airships.
- **Current Situation:**
    - The Artisanate continues to lead the world in engineering, transport and scientific discovery.
    - Debate continues over whether revolutionary inventions should belong to all or remain the property of their creators.
    - While its relationship with the Elven Ascendancy remains strong, increasing political tension surrounds technological independence and the sharing of knowledge.
    - Relations with [[The Watchers]] remain respectful but strained, as both nations continue to disagree over how dangerous knowledge should be studied and safeguarded.
    - The Last Equation continues to investigate existential threats to all life, many of which remain hidden from the wider world.

## Government & Military

- **Political Structure:**
    - Chancellor
    - Grand Assembly
    - Regional Representatives
    - Guild Delegates
    - Civic Councils
- **Military Overview:**
    - Comparatively small professional military.
    - Defensive fortifications built into mountains and cliffside settlements.
    - Highly advanced engineering corps supporting both civilian and military infrastructure.
    - Recently established airship fleet for reconnaissance, transport and exploration.
    - Citizens are encouraged to contribute through invention and logistics as much as direct military service.
- **Laws or Unique Customs:**
    - Education is freely available to all citizens.
    - Dangerous experimentation is strictly regulated following the Soulfracture.
    - Major discoveries are presented before the Grand Assembly for public record.
    - Every significant invention, discovery and failed experiment is permanently archived within the Grand Library of Asterreach.

## Culture

- **Values:**
    - Curiosity
    - Possibility
    - Responsibility
    - Innovation
    - Education
- **Traditions/Festivals:**
    - Public exhibitions of new inventions.
    - Celebrations honouring great explorers, inventors and astronomers.
    - The Day of Reflection commemorating the Soulfracture.
    - Annual astronomical festivals beneath the winter stars.
- **Everyday Life:**
    - Education forms the foundation of gnomish society, with libraries, workshops and observatories serving as the heart of nearly every settlement. The Grand Library of Asterreach seeks to preserve knowledge from every civilisation, not merely the history of the gnomes, making it the greatest collection of learning in the known world. Inventors, explorers, astronomers, teachers and engineers are celebrated for the contributions they leave behind, their achievements meticulously recorded so future generations can continue building upon them. Cities constantly evolve as new inventions are woven into older architecture, creating a landscape where centuries of progress can be read through stone, timber, brass and modern engineering. Failure is not considered shameful, but an essential part of discovery, provided its lessons are remembered. This philosophy was forged by the Soulfracture, ensuring that curiosity is forever balanced by responsibility.

## Diplomacy

- **Allies:**
	- [[The Elven Ascendancy]]
	- [[Rathuun]]
	- [[The Silver Dawn]]

- **Current Conflicts:**
	- Balancing technological progress with the ethical responsibilities of invention.
	- Ongoing political debate over whether transformative discoveries should be freely shared or privately controlled.
	- Maintaining independence while remaining a close protectorate of the [[The Elven Ascendancy]].
	- Supporting [[The Last Equation]] as it investigates existential threats to life, including the cosmic entity [[Xytherion]].
	- Navigating an increasingly strained philosophical relationship with [[The Watchers]]. Once a single civilisation, the two nations have gradually diverged over centuries, with the Artisanate believing dangerous knowledge should be responsibly understood and the Watchers believing some discoveries are simply too dangerous to pursue. Though mutual respect remains, this difference in philosophy continues to shape their relationship, particularly regarding [[Aidrix]] and other ancient relics.
