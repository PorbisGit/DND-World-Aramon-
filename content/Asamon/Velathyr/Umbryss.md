**Religion(s):** Worships the land and minor deities like [[Essera]]

## Overview

_Umbryss is the vast subterranean homeland of the dark elves and deep gnomes, and one of the protectorates of [[The Elven Ascendancy]]. Beneath [[Velathyr]], an immense network of caverns, crystal forests, underground rivers and ancient fungal groves stretches far beyond the reach of any single ruler. Over countless generations, its people have been shaped by the mysterious magic that permeates the deep places of the world, giving rise to a culture that values patience, wisdom and remembrance above conquest or ambition. At the heart of Umbryss lies [[The Eternal Weave]], a living web spun from the enchanted silk of [[Essera]], the Spider Queen, where memories are preserved instead of written, allowing future generations to experience the past rather than simply read about it. Though fiercely loyal to the Ascendancy, Umbryss also serves as its conscience, forever reminding the Empire of the arrogance that brought about the fall of the [[Yssar]]._

## History

- **Founding:**
    - Following the fall of the [[Yssar]], many elves and deep gnomes chose to remain beneath the surface, where centuries living amongst the ancient caverns gradually forged a distinct culture. Though they remained part of the wider elven civilisation, Umbryss would eventually become a protectorate of [[The Elven Ascendancy]], retaining its own customs, leadership and traditions.
- **Major Historical Events:**
    - Founding of Umbryss.
    - The weaving of [[The Eternal Weave]].
    - Rise of the Memory Weavers.
    - Establishment of the Keepers of the Silk.
    - Formal recognition as a protectorate of [[The Elven Ascendancy]].
- **Current Situation:**
    - Umbryss remains one of the most politically influential protectorates within the Empire.
    - [[The Eternal Weave]] continues to grow as each generation entrusts its memories to future descendants.
    - Strange ancient magic still lingers throughout the deepest caverns, though few seek to understand it.
    - Umbryss frequently acts as a moderating voice within Imperial politics, cautioning against repeating the mistakes of the past.

## Government & Military

- **Political Structure:**
    - Archon
    - Great Houses
    - Provincial Lords
    - Protectorate Council
    - Memory Weavers
- **Military Overview:**
    - Highly disciplined professional military.
    - Specialists in subterranean warfare and defensive operations.
    - Extensive tunnel fortifications connecting major settlements.
    - The Keepers of the Silk serve as elite monastic guardians of [[The Eternal Weave]].
    - Provincial forces are maintained by each of the Great Houses, allowing Umbryss to rapidly defend multiple regions simultaneously.
- **Laws or Unique Customs:**
    - Each Great House governs its own province with considerable autonomy, owing loyalty to the Archon while managing its lands much like a small kingdom.
    - Memories deemed historically significant are preserved within [[The Eternal Weave]].
    - Deliberately destroying or altering preserved memories is considered one of the greatest crimes in Umbryss.
    - Important political decisions often begin with consultation of memories preserved within [[The Eternal Weave]].

## Culture

- **Values:**
    - Memory
    - Wisdom
    - Patience
    - Responsibility
    - Community
- **Traditions/Festivals:**
    - Ceremonies preserving memories within [[The Eternal Weave]].
    - Annual remembrance honouring those whose memories have been entrusted to the Weave.
    - Silent pilgrimages to [[The Eternal Weave]].
    - Recognition of the Keepers of the Silk for their lifelong service.
- **Everyday Life:**
    - Memory forms the foundation of Umbryss' society. Rather than preserving history through books alone, the people entrust important experiences to the Memory Weavers, who carefully weave them into [[The Eternal Weave]] using the enchanted silk of **[[Essera]], the Spider Queen**. Citizens believe wisdom comes not simply from knowledge, but from understanding the experiences of those who came before them. The Great Houses rule their provinces with considerable independence, fostering strong local identities while remaining united beneath the Archon. Daily life is quiet and communal, with respect earned through service, judgement and the legacy one leaves for future generations. Heroes are remembered not because their names are written in history, but because others may one day walk beside them through the memories they chose to preserve.
