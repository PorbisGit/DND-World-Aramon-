## Overview

_Fervaar is a vast and untamed continent near the equator, where ancient ecosystems continue to thrive much as they did in ages long past. It is regarded as the ancestral homeland of dragons and many reptilian peoples, its wilderness stretching from dense tropical jungles and sprawling marshes to endless savannas and one of the largest deserts in [[Asamon]]. While much of the continent remains wild, ancient ruins and forgotten holy sites hint at civilisations and gods long lost to history. In recent years, the growing threat of the [[Fractured Ones]] has begun to scar the land itself, threatening not only Fervaar but the boundary between the living world and the Ethereal._

## History

**Origin:**

- Fervaar is one of the oldest continually inhabited lands in [[Asamon]], serving as the ancestral homeland of dragons and many reptilian races.
- Countless forgotten civilisations once flourished here, leaving behind temples, monuments and ruins dedicated to gods no longer widely remembered.

**Current State:**

- Much of the continent remains unexplored by outsiders.
- Tribal societies, isolated settlements and scattered colonies are separated by vast stretches of dangerous wilderness.
- The [[Fractured Ones]] continue to create mysterious wounds in reality, allowing the dead to cross into the living world and draining the life from the land around them.

## Culture

**Lifestyle:**

Life in Fervaar is shaped by the wilderness. Many societies live in tribes, clans or small settlements, relying on generations of knowledge to survive amongst enormous predators and unforgiving environments. Strength, adaptability and respect for nature are valued across much of the continent, though customs vary greatly between its peoples.
