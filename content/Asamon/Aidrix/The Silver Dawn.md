## Basic Information

**Name:** The Silver Dawn

**Type:** Colonial Administration

**Location:** [[Aidrix]], [[Asamon]], [[Material Plane]]

**Leader(s):** Overseer Syvis Miralee

**Associated Culture/Race:** The main races are [[Elves]] but occasionally [[Gnomes]] and [[Orcs]] too. It is ultimately own by [[The Elven Ascendancy]] who's puppet is [[The Artisanate]] which is why [[Gnomes]] are there too.

## Overview

*The Silver Dawn is the colonial administration of the Elven Ascendancy on Aidrix, established to secure ancient knowledge, valuable resources, and strategic influence before rival powers can claim them.*

## History

**Origin:** Founded as a competitor to pre-existing factions on [[Aidrix]] in the race to acquire everything the fallen empire has to offer.

**Major Events:** Discovered a vault containing a rare magical crystal capable of storing vast amounts of arcane energy, revolutionising magical engineering within the Ascendancy.

**Current Situation:** The Silver Dawn rivals are rapidly gaining power and cannot afford to fall behind. But with rapid growth comes mistakes and with mistakes comes opportunity.

## Beliefs & Identity

**Core Values:** Loyalty to the Empire, Discretion and Knowledge.

**Traditions:** Senior officials must be citizens of the Elven Ascendancy, and all members are expected to return to the homeland at least once every decade to renew their oath of service.

**Reputation:** While the Silver Dawn isn't feared amongst rival factions it's backer [[The Elven Ascendancy]] is.

**Common Symbols:** A silver crest of the dawning sun.

## Structure

**Leadership:** Led by Overseer Syvis Miralee, who is appointed directly by the Elven Ascendancy and advised by a council of senior administrators, military officers, and scholars.

**Ranks/Hierarchy:** Initiate → Officer → Senior Officer → Magistrate → High Magistrate → Overseer

**Important Members:** Overseer Syvis Miralee

## Resources & Influence

**Strengths:** Exceptional logistics, disciplined soldiers, experienced scholars, political influence, and direct support from the Elven Ascendancy.

**Resources:** Imperial funding, colonial settlements, research facilities, military garrisons, trade vessels, and recovered relics awaiting transport to the homeland.

**Areas of Influence:** [[Aidrix]]

**Allies:** [[The Elven Ascendancy]], [[The Artisanate]]

**Enemies/Rivals:** [[Rift Hunters]], [[The United Helm]], [[The Watchers]], [[The Sundered Lands Guild]]

## Goals & Conflicts

**Current Goals:** Expand the Ascendancy's colonial presence, secure ancient relics and magical knowledge, and establish dominance over Aidrix before rival factions can do the same.

**Internal Problems:** Growing tensions between military officials seeking rapid expansion and scholars who wish to carefully preserve the ancient knowledge they uncover.

**External Threats:** Fierce competition from rival factions, the ever-present dangers of Aidrix, and the possibility of open conflict should the Ascendancy's ambitions become too aggressive.
