## Basic Information

**Name:** Rift Hunters

**Type:** Mercenary Guild

**Location:** [[Aidrix]], [[Asamon]], [[Material Plane]]

**Leader(s):** Lord Hunter Ergoth Steelbreaker

**Associated Culture/Race:** All races

## Overview

*The Rift Hunters are a renowned mercenary guild dedicated to protecting the world from the horrors of Aidrix, undertaking hunts that few others would dare face.*

## History

**Origin:** The Rift Hunters were founded by a coalition of organisations to eliminate the aberrations emerging from [[Aidrix|The Island of the First Mages]] after they began threatening neighbouring continents.

**Major Events:** Slayed the Kraken, the largest aquatic beast the world had seen, which had been cutting off all supplies to [[Aidrix]].

**Current Situation:** The recent war in [[Ogrin]] and the increasing ambition of other factions have lead tensions and uncertainty to an all time high.

## Beliefs & Identity

**Core Values:** Strength, Victory and Self Sacrifice.

**Traditions:** New recruits swear an oath to hunt, protect and cut off any ties to their past or hopes for the future.

**Reputation:** A diverse mix of elite mercenaries that always finish their hunt.

**Common Symbols:** A hunting bow with a notched arrow over symbolism of a rift.

## Structure

**Leadership:** Led by the Lord Hunter supported by elder hunters chosen for strength, experience and wisdom.

**Ranks/Hierarchy:** Recruit -> Hunter -> Senior Hunter -> Elder Hunter -> Lord Hunter

**Important Members:** Lord Hunter Ergoth Steelbreaker

## Resources & Influence

**Strengths:** Highly trained mercenaries and a large navy all equipped with unique designed gear and funded by various governments and organisations.

**Resources:** Well-equipped armoury, supply boats and various outposts around [[Aidrix]].

**Areas of Influence:** Are a force all throughout [[Aidrix]]

**Allies:** [[The Sundered Lands Guild]], [[The United Helm]], [[Rathuun]], [[Dwarves|Some Dwarven Houses]]

**Enemies/Rivals:** [[The Silver Dawn]] and [[The Watchers]]


## Goals & Conflicts

**Current Goals:** Destroy all the monstrosities and horror from [[Aidrix]]

**Internal Problems:** Disagreements with some of the organisation that are funding them.

**External Threats:** Constantly bashing heads with other groups who seek their own purpose
