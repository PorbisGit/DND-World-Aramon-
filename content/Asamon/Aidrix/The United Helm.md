**Associated Culture/Race:** [[Humans]] 

## Overview

The United Helm is an alliance born from the combined colonial outposts of three human kingdoms. Once united by their rulers, they now stand alone, struggling to forge a new identity after the collapse of the alliance that created them.

## History

**Origin:** After the King of [[Stanosta]] formed an alliance with the leaders of [[Vibet]] & [[Stolan]] uniting all the humans for a time, one part of the agreement was the consolidation of the three kingdoms [[Aidrix]] outposts.

**Major Events:** The Unification of the United Helm meant there was a new major player on [[Aidrix]]. The death of the king of [[Stanosta]] and the crumbling of the human alliance caused [[The United Helm]] to come fully independent.

**Current Situation:** Now that the Helm is independent they must organise themselves in this uncertain time.

## Beliefs & Identity

**Core Values:** Once built upon _Honour, Prosperity, and Understanding_, many now question whether these ideals can survive without the alliance that gave them meaning.

**Traditions:** New recruits are welcomed through the Festival of Three Banners, celebrating the unity of the kingdoms that founded the Helm.

**Reputation:** Seen as a faction with great potential but little direction, many believe its future depends on whether it can unite under strong leadership before its rivals exploit its weakness.

**Common Symbols:** A Great Helm adorned with the symbols of the Three Kingdoms.

## Structure

**Leadership:** Governed by a provisional council representing the three founding kingdoms until a permanent leader can be chosen.

**Ranks/Hierarchy:** Recruit → Ranger → Captain → Commander → High Commander

## Resources & Influence

**Strengths:** Experienced settlers, defensible settlements, and a population determined to preserve the alliance despite overwhelming hardship.

**Resources:** Established settlements, defensive fortifications, modest naval fleets, skilled craftsmen, and dwindling stockpiles inherited from the three kingdoms.

**Areas of Influence:** [[Aidrix]]

**Allies:** [[Rift Hunters]]

**Enemies/Rivals:** [[The Watchers]], [[The Silver Dawn]], [[The Sundered Lands Guild]]

## Goals & Conflicts

**Current Goals:** Establish a stable government, secure new alliances, and find a sustainable future before dwindling supplies force the faction to dissolve.

**Internal Problems:** Political division, food shortages, and uncertainty over the faction's future.

**External Threats:** Rival factions seek to absorb the United Helm before it can recover, while the dangers of Aidrix leave little time to rebuild.
