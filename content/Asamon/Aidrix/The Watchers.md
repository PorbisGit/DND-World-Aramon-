## Basic Information

**Name:** The Watchers

**Type:** Custodial Technocracy

**Location:** [[Aidrix]], [[Asamon]], [[Material Plane]]

**Leader(s):** 

**Associated Culture/Race:** [[Gnomes]]

## Overview

*The Watchers are a secretive society of gnomish scholars, researchers, and guardians who believe the stability of Aidrix is more important than the ambitions of any nation. Possessing knowledge few others can comprehend, they work tirelessly to contain threats before they can spread beyond the island.*

## History

**Origin:** First Faction on [[Aidrix]] founded as a research branch of the [[The Artisanate]].

**Major Events:** First to rediscover [[Aidrix]] after its collapse. Manage to decipher how the Caretakers work and create their own automatons.

**Current Situation:** Now an independent power, the Watchers have tasked themselves alone to maintain the balance of [[Aidrix]] so that the world doesn't end.

## Beliefs & Identity

**Core Values:** Preservation and Stability at all costs. 

**Traditions:** Members periodically undergo psychological and magical evaluations to ensure the influence of [[Aidrix]] has not compromised their minds.

**Reputation:** To many they are secretive, arrogant, and needlessly controlling. To those who understand Aidrix's true dangers, however, the Watchers may be the only reason the island has not already consumed itself.

**Common Symbols:** A eye with similarities to a clock embedded into a tower. 

## Structure

**Leadership:** Governed by the Council of Watchers, a small assembly of the faction's brightest scientists, mages, and historians, each chosen for their expertise rather than political influence.

**Ranks/Hierarchy:** Apprentice → Scholar → Curator → Warden → High Warden → Grand Watcher

## Resources & Influence

**Strengths:** Unmatched understanding of Aidrix, advanced magical research, powerful automatons, exceptional intelligence gathering, and centuries of accumulated knowledge.

**Resources:** Self-sustaining settlements, ancient research facilities, caretaker automatons, extensive archives, and technologies unknown to the outside world.

**Areas of Influence:** [[Aidrix]]

**Allies:** Formerly [[The Artisanate]] when they were independent.

**Enemies/Rivals:** [[The Elven Ascendancy]], [[Rift Hunters]], [[The United Helm]], [[The Silver Dawn]], [[The Sundered Lands Guild]]

## Goals & Conflicts

**Current Goals:** Preserve the balance of Aidrix, contain its greatest dangers, and prevent the island's secrets from falling into reckless hands.

**Internal Problems:** Curiosity often outweighs caution, leading some Watchers to experiment with dangerous magic, ancient relics, and entities that should never be disturbed.

**External Threats:** Every major faction covets the Watchers' knowledge, while their refusal to share it has made them enemies across Aidrix.
