## Basic Information

**Name:** Sundered Lands Guild

**Type:** General Guild

**Location:** [[Aidrix]], [[Asamon]], [[Material Plane]]

**Leader(s):** Guild master Mardoum Stronghelm

**Associated Culture/Race:** [[Dwarves]], [[Khazrim]]

## Overview

*The Sundered Lands Guild is a powerful merchant and expedition guild that explores Aidrix in search of ancient relics, rare resources, and forgotten knowledge, having grown into one of Asamon's wealthiest and most influential organizations.*

## History

**Origin:** Founded by [[Khazrim]] to reap the rewards of [[Aidrix]], that mostly being rare artifacts and money.

**Major Events:** Uncovered a vault which granted the world knowledge of gunpowder and firearms making the guild the leading manufacturer and purveyor of guns and explosives.

**Current Situation:** Now armed with powerful weapons and lots of money, the guild has started to throw their weight around all of [[Asamon]].

## Beliefs & Identity

**Core Values:** Wealth, Opportunity and Legacy

**Traditions:** All members must contribute something to help the recovery of the lost Stoneways of [[Khazrim]].

**Reputation:** A morally grey merchant group seeking to make more money than anyone else but had once honourable roots.

**Common Symbols:** A pickaxe cracking a rare gem.

## Structure

**Leadership:** Guild master Mardoum Stronghelm, [[Khazrim]]

**Ranks/Hierarchy:** Apprentice → Merchant → Senior Merchant → Factor → Guild Elder → Guild Master

**Important Members:** Guild master Mardoum Stronghelm

## Resources & Influence

**Strengths:** A large trade fleet and a dominance in the market of firearms and explosives

**Resources:** Extensive wealth, merchant networks across Asamon, a powerful trade fleet, control over the firearm and explosives industry, and numerous mining and excavation operations.

**Areas of Influence:** Typically [[Aidrix]], [[Khazrim]] and [[Ogrin]] but recently all across [[Asamon]]

**Allies:** [[Khazrim]], [[Rift Hunters]]

**Enemies/Rivals:** [[The Watchers]], [[The Silver Dawn]], [[The United Helm]]

## Goals & Conflicts

**Current Goals:** Grow their market share and become fully independent.

**Internal Problems:** While current management is trying to grow into something new, many still believe the must honour their ancestors by sticking to their original goal of helping the recovery of the Stoneways.

**External Threats:** Too much internal conflict might lead to an attack by [[The Silver Dawn]] and or [[The United Helm]]
