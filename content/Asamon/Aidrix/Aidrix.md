## Basic Information

**Name:** Aidrix

**Type:** Island Continent

**Region/Continent:** [[Asamon]], [[Material Plane]]

**Population:** Unknown (small permanent population, many temporary explorers and settlers)

**Primary Inhabitants:** [[Humans]], [[Elves]], [[Gnomes]], [[Dwarves]], Automatons, Aberrations, and other magical creatures

## Overview

_Also known as the Island of the First Mages, Aidrix was once home to the world's greatest magical empire. After its inhabitants pushed the boundaries of magic beyond what reality could withstand, the island was destroyed by a catastrophe that shattered time and space itself._

_Now, Aidrix is a dead and unstable land where reality behaves unpredictably. Ancient ruins shift between different points in history, landscapes change without warning, and forgotten vaults containing unimaginable knowledge and dangers remain scattered across the island. Despite its dangers, countless factions continue to explore Aidrix in search of lost magic, powerful artefacts, and secrets from the world's greatest magical civilisation._

## Geography

- **Climate:** Harsh and unnatural. Weather patterns change unpredictably, with some regions experiencing impossible conditions such as frozen deserts, endless storms, or areas trapped in repeating weather cycles.
- **Terrain:** A fractured landscape of ruined cities, warped wilderness, magical wastelands, and regions where time and space have become unstable.
- **Natural Features:**
    - Areas where time flows differently.
    - Floating or distorted landscapes.
    - Ancient magical scars left from the destruction of the First Mages.
    - Reality fractures that connect to unknown places.

## Settlements

- **The Watcher's Enclave**  
    The hidden research settlement of [[The Watchers]], built around ancient First Mage facilities. It serves as the faction's primary base of operations and contains their archives, laboratories, and caretaker research stations.
- **The Silver Dawn Citadel**  
    The main colonial settlement of [[The Silver Dawn]], representing the influence of [[The Elven Ascendancy]] on Aidrix. A heavily organised outpost focused on research, resource extraction, and securing ancient knowledge.
- **The United Helm**  
    A network of human settlements established from the combined outposts of [[Stanosta]], [[Vibet]], and [[Stolan]]. Now independent, they struggle to maintain their population and infrastructure without the support of their former kingdoms.
- **The Sundered Lands Hold**  
    The primary settlement of [[The Sundered Lands Guild]], acting as a centre for trade, exploration, and manufacturing. It houses their workshops, vaults, and operations for recovering and selling Aidrix's resources.
- **Rift Hunter Bastion**  
    The main fortress of [[Rift Hunters]], serving as a military headquarters, training ground, and staging point for expeditions against Aidrix's monsters and aberrations.

## History

**Origin:**  
Aidrix was once the heart of the First Mage Empire, a civilisation that achieved magical advancement beyond any known society. Their experiments eventually surpassed the limits of reality, causing the destruction of their empire and the corruption of the island.

**Notable Events:**

- The rise of the First Mage Empire.
- The creation of impossible magical technologies.
- The Cataclysm that shattered time and space.
- The rediscovery of Aidrix by [[The Watchers]].
- The arrival of competing factions seeking its secrets.

**Current State:**  
Aidrix remains an unstable and largely unexplored land. Several factions compete for control and knowledge, while the island's dangers continue to grow and change.

## Culture

**Lifestyle:**  
Life on Aidrix revolves around survival, exploration, and adaptation. Settlers rely heavily on imported supplies, ancient technology, and careful planning due to the inability for normal life to thrive.

**Traditions:**  
Explorers often record their discoveries, mark safe paths, and leave warnings for those who follow. Many groups develop rituals or practices to protect themselves from Aidrix's effects on the mind and body.
