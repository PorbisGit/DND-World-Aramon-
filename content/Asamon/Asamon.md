## Overview

_Asamon is a world built upon the foundations of countless civilisations that have risen and fallen throughout history. From towering empires and ancient kingdoms to forgotten gods and lost wonders, every age has left its mark upon the world. Today, during the [[Age of Revelation]], long-buried secrets are beginning to resurface as new powers emerge and old threats stir once more. Across every continent, the choices made by its people will determine whether the current age becomes the beginning of a lasting legacy or another forgotten chapter in history._
