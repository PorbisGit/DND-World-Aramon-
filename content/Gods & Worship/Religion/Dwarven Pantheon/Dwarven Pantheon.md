## Overview

_The Honoured are the revered deities of dwarfkind, embodying the ideals upon which dwarven civilisation was founded. At their head stands Brakkir, the creator of the dwarves, while the others are legendary heroes whose lives, deeds and unwavering devotion earned them ascension after death. Together, they represent the virtues that every dwarf strives to uphold throughout their life._

_Unlike many faiths, the dwarven pantheon teaches that greatness is achieved through one's actions rather than birth. Every craft perfected, every promise honoured, every life defended and every new path discovered reflects the teachings left behind by the Honoured._

## Beliefs

**Core Teachings:**

- Mastery is earned through patience and perseverance.
    
- Honour is measured by actions, not words.
    
- Protect those who cannot protect themselves.
    
- Preserve knowledge for future generations.
    
- Face the unknown with courage and curiosity.

**View of the Gods:**

The Honoured are revered as both divine beings and the greatest examples of what a dwarf can become. Brakkir is honoured as the creator and father of dwarfkind, while the remaining Honoured are remembered as legendary dwarves whose deeds earned them a place among the divine.

**Important Values:**

- Honour

- Unity

**View of Life and Death:**

An honourable life is one lived in service to others and dedicated to leaving the world better than it was found. The Honoured teach that every deed contributes to the legacy a dwarf leaves behind, and that true greatness is measured by the lives one shapes rather than the wealth one possesses.

## Worship

**Common Practices:**

Daily prayers are often offered before beginning a craft, embarking on a journey, taking up arms or making an important decision. Dwarves commonly invoke the Honoured whose teachings best reflect the task before them, though Brakkir is honoured by nearly all as the creator of dwarfkind.
