**Divine Rank:** Intermediate Deity

**Pantheon:** [[Dwarven Pantheon|The Honoured]]

## Overview

_Thargrim is the patron of exploration, discovery and courageous endeavour. Headstrong, light-hearted and fearless in the face of the unknown, he believed that every horizon held the promise of new knowledge. While many remember him for guiding the survivors safely out of the collapsing Stoneways, his greatest legacy was refusing to let fear confine dwarfkind. He later led the great effort to reclaim and recover the Stoneways, inspiring generations to push beyond the safety of what was known._

_Although his origins are rooted beneath the mountains, Thargrim's teachings extend far beyond them. To his followers, exploration is not simply the act of delving into caves, but the pursuit of discovery wherever it may be found—across mountains, forests, oceans, deserts and every corner of Asamon. Every journey into the unknown is an opportunity to learn, grow and leave the world better understood than before._

## Influence

**Followers:**

Thargrim is revered by miners, explorers, adventurers, cartographers, scouts, sailors, rangers and warriors who venture into the unknown. Many offer a prayer before setting out on a journey, asking for courage, safe passage and the wisdom to discover what lies beyond the horizon.

**Associated Places:**

- The recovered passages of the Stoneways.
    
- The great trade routes and unexplored frontiers of Asamon.
    

**Relationships:**
