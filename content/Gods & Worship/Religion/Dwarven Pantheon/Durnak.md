## Basic Information

**Name:** [[Durnak]]

**Titles:**

- The Stone Shield
    
- Guardian of Dwarfkind
    
- The Last Defender

**Divine Rank:** Lesser Deity

**Pantheon:** [[Dwarven Pantheon|The Honoured]]

## Overview

*Durnak is the steadfast protector of dwarfkind and the embodiment of sacrifice, resilience and unwavering courage. When the Stoneways began to collapse beneath an impossibly large horde of monsters, he stood alone to defend the surviving dwarves, buying them the time they needed to escape. Though he fell in the battle, his selfless sacrifice became one of the defining moments in dwarven history, earning him a place among the Honoured.*

*Remembered as strong, fierce and stubborn, Durnak teaches that true strength is measured not by the battles won, but by those who are protected.*

## Domains

- Protection
    
- Sacrifice
    
- Warriors

**Symbol:**

A circular stone shield.

**Sacred Object:**

Durnak's Stone Shield, said to have been carved from a single piece of unbreakable stone.

## Teachings

**Core Virtues:**

- Courage
    
- Duty
    
- Selflessness
    
- Resolve
    
- Loyalty

**Lessons:**

- Stand firm, even when defeat seems certain.
    
- Strength exists to protect others.
    
- Duty outweighs fear.
    
- A shield raised for another is never carried alone.
    
- No wall is stronger than those willing to defend it.

## Influence

**Followers:**

Durnak is revered by warriors, guards, soldiers, city watch, bodyguards and all who dedicate themselves to defending others. Many seek his guidance before battle, while legends tell of Durnak himself appearing to train exceptional warriors whose resolve matches his own.

**Associated Places:**

- The site of his final stand within the Stoneways.
  
**Relationships:**

- One of [[Dwarven Pantheon|The Honoured]].
    
- Defender of dwarfkind during the collapse of the Stoneways.
