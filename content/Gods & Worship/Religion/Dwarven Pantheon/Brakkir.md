## Basic Information

**Name:** [[Brakkir]]

**Titles:**

- The First Smith
    
- Father of Dwarfkind
    
- Keeper of the Forge of Creation

**Divine Rank:** Overdeity

**Pantheon:** [[Dwarven Pantheon|The Honoured]]

## Overview

*Brakkir is the creator of dwarfkind and the first of the Honoured. According to dwarven tradition, he forged the first dwarves from living stone within the legendary Forge of Creation, gifting them life and the gift of craftsmanship. To the dwarves, every act of honest creation is a reflection of Brakkir's first work, and every craft is considered a sacred inheritance from their creator.*

*Patient, compassionate and steadfast, Brakkir is remembered not only as the greatest craftsman to have ever existed, but as the loving father of all dwarves. He teaches that mastery is achieved through perseverance, that every failure is an opportunity to learn, and that a life's worth is measured by what it leaves behind.*

**Lessons:**

## Influence

**Followers:**

All dwarves honour Brakkir as their creator, though he is especially revered by blacksmiths, artisans, masons, engineers, jewellers, architects and apprentices seeking to master their chosen craft.
