**Pantheon:** [[Dwarven Pantheon|The Honoured]]

## Overview

_Khordun was the first king to unite the dwarven clans beneath a single crown, leading the Kingdom of Old into an age of prosperity and greatness. Renowned for his honour, unwavering resolve and devotion to his people, he established many of the traditions, laws and oaths that continue to shape dwarven society to this day._

_Following his death, Khordun became the first dwarf to ascend to godhood, proving that a mortal life lived with unwavering honour and duty could earn a place among the divine. As the highest of the ascended Honoured, he stands as the ideal ruler, teaching that true leadership is measured not by power, but by the trust and unity it inspires._

## Influence

**Followers:**

Khordun is revered by kings, queens, clan leaders, generals, judges, diplomats and oath bearers. Though many seek his guidance in positions of leadership, he is honoured by dwarves of every walk of life as the first king and the first mortal to ascend among [[Dwarven Pantheon|The Honoured]].
