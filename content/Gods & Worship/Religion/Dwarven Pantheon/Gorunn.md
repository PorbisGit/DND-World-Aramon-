# Gorunn

## Basic Information

**Name:** [[Gorunn]]

**Titles:**

- The Keeper of Lore
    
- Warden of the Timeless Library
    
- The Eternal Scholar

**Divine Rank:** Lesser Deity

**Pantheon:** [[Dwarven Pantheon|The Honoured]]

## Overview

_Gorunn is the guardian of knowledge, wisdom and tradition. Wise, calculating and cautious, she believed that a civilisation's greatest strength was not its armies or wealth, but the knowledge it preserved and the promises it kept. As the keeper of the legendary Timeless Library, she devoted her life to recording the achievements, history and traditions of dwarfkind so they would never be forgotten._

_Although the Timeless Library was ultimately lost beneath the Stoneways, dwarves believe its countless volumes still remain preserved in the darkness, waiting to be rediscovered. Gorunn teaches that wisdom comes through patience, truth and understanding, and that knowledge carries the responsibility to guide others._

## Influence

**Followers:**

Gorunn is revered by scholars, advisors, historians, scribes, judges, teachers and librarians. Those entrusted with preserving knowledge or offering counsel often seek her guidance before making important decisions.
